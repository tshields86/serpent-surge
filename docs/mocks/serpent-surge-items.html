FOOD TYPES

Basic food. Eat to grow and score.

+50 PTS · +1 LENGTH
Rare and valuable. Pulsing gold glow.
SHRINK PELLET
+25 PTS · −2 LENGTH
Makes you shorter. Great for tight spaces.

+15 PTS · 1.5× SPEED
Temporary speed boost for 3 seconds.
BOMB FRUIT
+30 PTS · CLEARS HAZARDS
Destroys hazards in a 3×3 area.
HOW TO PLAY
HAZARDS
WALL BLOCK DEADLY
Static obstacle. Destroyed by Bomb Fruit or Head Bash.
SPIKE TRAP DEADLY
Toggles every few ticks. Safe when retracted, deadly when red.
POISON TRAIL DEADLY
Fades after 8 ticks. Avoid the purple glow until it clears.
WARP HOLE NEUTRAL
Comes in pairs. Enter one, exit the other. Disorienting, not deadly.
MAGNET NEUTRAL
Pulls nearby food toward it each tick. Plan your route.

Food = soft rounded silhouettes · hazards = hard / spiky shapes · Warp Hole moved from purple to cyan so purple now means “poison, deadly” only · DEADLY vs NEUTRAL tags tell players what actually kills them
